How to get the most out of it

A few habits that make answers noticeably better.

Be specific

“Sharp pain on the inside edge of my shoulder blade when I sit at a desk” retrieves far better evidence than “back pain”.

Ask follow-up questions

The conversation keeps context, so you can drill into a mechanism, ask for clarification, or explore a related pattern.

Open the evidence drawers

Under each answer, Sources and Evidence used show exactly which articles and passages the answer is based on.

Check “Why this answer?”

Retrieval confidence, timing, and the refined query are shown for every response — transparency over polish.

Try Mechanics Study mode

A focused mode for studying mechanisms from the articles — nerves, entrapment sites, and muscle relationships. Interpretation only, never treatment planning.

Start from a region or topic

Not sure how to phrase it? The chat opens with body-region shortcuts and curated clinical topics the evidence base covers in depth.

When not to use this

This is educational triage support, not medical advice. For any of the following, seek urgent in-person care instead:

  • New or worsening weakness or numbness
  • Bowel or bladder changes
  • Major trauma with concerning symptoms
  • Fever with feeling very unwell
  • Unexplained weight loss with persistent pain
  • Severe chest pain or trouble breathing

These patterns are screened for deterministically on every message, before any AI generation runs. If one is detected, the assistant stops and directs you to in-person care.

Common questions

Is this medical advice?

No. MSK Assist is an educational tool that explains biomechanical and neurological patterns described in its evidence base. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace evaluation by a clinician.

Is anything I type stored?

No. The app runs in guest-only mode — conversations live in your browser session and are gone when you close it. There are no accounts and no saved history.

Where do the answers come from?

From a curated corpus of musculoskeletal neurology articles, indexed into 1,300+ evidence passages. The assistant retrieves the most relevant passages for your question and composes its answer only from them, citing each source.

What happens if I describe something urgent?

Deterministic safety checks run on every message before anything else. If your description matches an urgent pattern — like new weakness, bowel or bladder changes, or chest pain — the assistant stops and directs you to in-person care rather than generating an answer.
