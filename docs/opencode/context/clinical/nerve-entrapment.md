# Clinical Context: Nerve Entrapment

Entrapment-like symptoms in this domain are often linked to posture, joint orientation, and regional space constraints.

Reasoning expectations:
- Distinguish plausible compression sites from generic assumptions.
- Avoid single-cause certainty when multiple mechanisms may coexist.
- Prefer evidence-supported mechanism explanations from retrieved context.

Communication expectations:
- Keep language non-alarmist for benign patterns.
- Escalate when progressive neurologic signs appear.
